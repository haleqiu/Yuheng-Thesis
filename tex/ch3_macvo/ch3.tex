\section{Introduction}

This chapter presents MAC-VO (Metrics-Aware Covariance for Stereo Visual Odometry), which won Best Paper Award at ICRA 2025. MAC-VO addresses the fundamental challenge of uncertainty estimation in stereo visual odometry by introducing learned covariance estimates that accurately reflect system reliability.

\section{Problem Formulation}

Traditional stereo visual odometry methods rely on hand-crafted uncertainty models that often fail to capture the true reliability of pose estimates. This leads to overconfident predictions in challenging scenarios and suboptimal performance in downstream fusion tasks.
