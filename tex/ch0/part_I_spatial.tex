{\LARGE \textbf{Learning Uncertainty from Individual Sensor Modalities}}

\vspace{2cm}

\begin{center}
\textit{This part establishes the foundation of learning-based uncertainty estimation by developing uncertainty models for individual perception modalities. We begin with visual perception (MAC-VO), progress to inertial sensing (AirIMU), and conclude with enhanced inertial odometry (AirIO). Each chapter demonstrates how learned uncertainty models outperform traditional hand-tuned parameters, setting the stage for multi-sensor fusion in Part II.}
\end{center}
