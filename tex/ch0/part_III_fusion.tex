\begin{center}
\textit{With visual and inertial uncertainty established in Parts I and II, this part couples the two streams through metrics-aware uncertainty for multi-modal fusion and adaptation. \textbf{MAC-I$^2$} (completed) uses metrics-aware covariance from both modalities for robust, tuning-free visual-inertial initialization and online calibration. \textbf{MACVIO} (proposed) extends this to cross-modal uncertainty correlation learning for adaptive fusion weights and sensor reliability handling. \textbf{MAC-IO} (proposed) uses EKF and IMU preintegration as structured self-supervision signals to adapt uncertainty-aware inertial models on real data, closing the loop between estimation structure and domain transfer.}
\end{center}
