\begin{center}
\textit{This part extends single-sensor uncertainty learning to multi-modal fusion scenarios. We first address \textbf{MAC-I$^2$}, robust VI initialization and calibration that bridges Part I to fusion. Building on the individual sensor models, we then develop cross-modal uncertainty correlations (MACVIO) and demonstrate cross-platform adaptation capabilities (MACIO). These chapters establish uncertainty as the theoretical foundation for optimal sensor fusion and enable deployment across diverse robotic platforms.}
\end{center}
