\begin{center}
\textit{Mammals complement vision with inertial sensing (vestibular and proprioceptive cues) to stabilize self-motion and orientation. Analogously, this part establishes learned inertial uncertainty as the second foundation. \textbf{AirIMU} jointly trains IMU noise correction and uncertainty propagation through a differentiable preintegration pipeline, producing metrics-aware inertial covariance that generalizes across IMU grades and robotic platforms. \textbf{AirIO} then shows that preserving body-frame IMU representation improves observability under agile UAV dynamics, and fuses the learned inertial model with an uncertainty-aware EKF for learning-based inertial odometry without external sensors. With visual and inertial uncertainty both established, the thesis proceeds to sensor fusion and adaptation.}
\end{center}
