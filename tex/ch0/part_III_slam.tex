\begin{center}
\textit{This part focuses on \textbf{uncertainty-aware SLAM}. Building on learned odometry and IMU uncertainty, we develop multi-sensor SLAM formulations that explicitly model measurement covariance, cross-factor correlations, and calibration drift. We investigate how uncertainty calibration impacts data association, loop closure, and robust optimization, and how these elements scale across environments and platforms.}
\end{center}
