\textit{This part establishes metrics-aware uncertainty for odometry. We first develop visual uncertainty and 2D$\rightarrow$3D covariance propagation in \textbf{MAC-VO}, then learn motion-adaptive IMU uncertainty in \textbf{AirIMU}, and finally enhance inertial odometry with uncertainty-aware models in \textbf{AirIO}. These chapters replace hand-tuned noise with learned, scale-consistent covariance for robust two-frame optimization, setting up multi-modal fusion next.}
