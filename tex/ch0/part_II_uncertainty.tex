\begin{center}
\textit{This part extends single-sensor uncertainty learning to multi-modal fusion scenarios. Building on the individual sensor models from Part I, we develop cross-modal uncertainty correlations (MACVIO) and demonstrate cross-platform adaptation capabilities (MACIO). These chapters establish uncertainty as the theoretical foundation for optimal sensor fusion and enable deployment across diverse robotic platforms.}
\end{center}
