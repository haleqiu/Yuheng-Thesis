\section{Introduction}

This chapter presents MACSLAM, a robust SLAM system that explicitly models multi-frame measurement covariance in the factor graph to improve feature selection, loop closure, and estimator consistency across environments and platforms. Building on the metrics-aware uncertainty from MAC-VO, AirIMU, and MACVIO, MACSLAM extends uncertainty learning to the full SLAM pipeline including multi-frame optimization and loop closure.
