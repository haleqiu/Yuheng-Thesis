\section{Introduction}

This chapter presents MACIO (Metrics-Aware Cross-Platform Inertial Odometry), which addresses the challenge of deploying learned uncertainty models across different robotic platforms. MACIO develops self-supervised domain transfer methods that enable zero-shot deployment with automatic sensor configuration.
